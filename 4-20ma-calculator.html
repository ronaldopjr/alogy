How to use the result

  1. Set your range. LRV is the value at 4 mA; URV is the value at 20 mA. Enter the unit of your process variable.
  2. Choose the value you know. Enter current, percentage or engineering value. The other values update automatically.
  3. Compare readings. In the field, compare measured current with the local indication and the PLC/DCS scaling.

Linear conversion formulas

Percentage = (mA − 4) ÷ 16 × 100
Value = LRV + (Percentage ÷ 100) × (URV − LRV)
mA = 4 + (Percentage ÷ 100) × 16

Example: converting 8.4 mA to bar

For a 0 to 10 bar transmitter: (8.4 − 4) ÷ 16 = 0.275. Therefore, 27.5% × 10 bar = 2.75 bar. Changing the configured range changes the engineering value represented by the same current.

Frequently asked questions

What does 12 mA represent?

12 mA is 50% of a linear 4-20 mA scale. For a 0 to 10 bar range, it represents 5 bar; for a −50 to 150 °C range, it represents 50 °C.

How do I convert engineering units to mA?

Subtract LRV from your value, divide by URV − LRV, multiply by 16 and add 4. In this calculator, select “Engineering value” as your input mode.

Can I use a reversed range?

Yes. If the value at 4 mA is higher than the value at 20 mA, the scale decreases. The calculator identifies the reversed range and preserves the correspondence of both endpoints.

What happens below 4 mA or above 20 mA?

The calculator shows a mathematical extrapolation and flags the out-of-range point. The slider remains limited to 4-20 mA. This does not diagnose a fault or saturation: diagnostic thresholds depend on the instrument and its configuration.

Does this work for square-root flow signals?

This calculator assumes the signal is linear with the selected variable. For flow, use it when the signal is already linearized in flow units. A signal that is linear in differential pressure requires square-root conversion to obtain flow.

Are the PDF and printed report branded?

No. Both actions produce a neutral report with your range, input, result, chart and table. The PDF downloads directly; printing opens a separate report. Calculation data is processed on your device.

Technical basis and limits

The relationship between LRV, URV and current is linear interpolation. This tool does not assess accuracy, uncertainty, power supply, HART load or installation integrity. It is not a calibration certificate. Check the transmitter and analog input manuals for the configured parameters.
